<TLDR>
  Separate capacity waits, startup, and execution when diagnosing query latency.
  Account for overlapping phases and compare cold and warm runs separately;
  faster operators may have little impact when startup dominates.
</TLDR>

## Start with a timeline

A query may wait for capacity, initialize workers, read metadata, and then execute operators. These phases can overlap; summing independent measurements can double-count time.

## Measure before concluding

Record timestamps at clear boundaries and compare warm and cold runs separately. A faster operator does not necessarily improve end-to-end latency when startup dominates.
